# ContractIQ

**AI-powered contract intelligence that saves mid-market teams 40+ hours per month while eliminating renewal surprises and compliance risks.**

## Executive Summary

ContractIQ is an AI-powered contract lifecycle management platform designed specifically for mid-market companies (500-5000 employees) managing 50-500 vendor contracts. Unlike enterprise CLM solutions that require months of implementation and cost $50K+ annually, ContractIQ delivers instant value with 10-minute setup, pre-trained AI models for common contract types, and pricing starting at $499/month. The platform's adaptive AI learns each company's contract preferences to provide increasingly personalized risk assessments, renewal recommendations, and negotiation guidance.

### Objectives
- Reduce contract review time by 70% (from 40-60 hours to 12-18 hours per month) within 90 days of implementation
- Achieve 95% contract renewal visibility with automated alerts 90, 60, and 30 days before renewal dates
- Capture $2M+ in savings per customer annually through optimized renewals and eliminated auto-renewals
- Onboard 150 mid-market customers in Year 1, achieving $900K ARR by Q4
- Achieve 90%+ customer satisfaction score and <5% monthly churn rate within 6 months of launch

### Success Metrics
- Time-to-value: 80% of customers extract first contract insights within 24 hours of signup
- Contract processing speed: Average 5 minutes per contract analysis (vs. 45-60 minutes manual review)
- Renewal capture rate: Customers identify and action 98%+ of upcoming renewals within first 90 days
- Cost savings: Average customer saves $167K annually (documented through avoided auto-renewals and better negotiated terms)
- Platform adoption: 75% weekly active usage rate among licensed users by Month 3
