# PolicyPilot

**Transform corporate policies from compliance burdens into intelligent, conversational knowledge that employees actually use.**

---

## Executive Summary

PolicyPilot is an AI-powered policy management platform that converts static policy documents into an interactive, intelligent knowledge system. Built for growing companies (200-5000 employees), it solves the critical problem of policy chaos—outdated documents, impossible navigation, zero engagement, and compliance nightmares. Unlike traditional document management systems, PolicyPilot proactively maintains policy health while delivering instant, accurate answers through conversational AI embedded in employees' daily tools (Slack, Teams, email).

### Objectives
- Reduce policy search time from days to seconds by providing instant, AI-powered answers with source citations
- Increase policy compliance awareness from <20% to >80% through proactive engagement and accessible information
- Cut policy creation and update cycles from 3-6 months to 2-4 weeks with collaborative workflows and automated conflict detection
- Eliminate audit preparation time by maintaining real-time compliance documentation and acknowledgment tracking
- Reduce legal/compliance team time spent answering policy questions by 70% through self-service AI assistance

### Success Metrics
- Average policy question response time: <30 seconds (baseline: 2-5 days) within 90 days of deployment
- Employee policy engagement rate: >60% monthly active users (baseline: <10%) by month 6
- Policy acknowledgment completion rate: >95% within 30 days (baseline: 60-70% within 90 days) by month 3
- Policy staleness reduction: <5% of policies outdated beyond review date (baseline: 40-60%) by month 12
- Customer Net Promoter Score (NPS): >50 among compliance/legal teams by month 6
